# Code of conduct

Be courteous and composed in every exchange here. That covers an issue,
a pull request, a discussion and a review.

- Tolerate a view you disagree with.
- Assume the other person means well. Text carries no tone.
- Keep personal attacks and disparaging remarks out of your words and
  your actions.
- We do not tolerate harassment.

Nobody here is a vendor. This is unpaid work, published in the hope that
it helps you. You are not entitled to an answer, a fix, a feature or a
date. A demand for one gets no reply.

Answer a provocation with silence. Somebody who wants an argument loses
nothing by getting one, and the reply is what everybody else remembers.
Where it is needed we lock a thread, delete a comment, or block an
account.

This holds for what you do here. It does not follow you anywhere else.
